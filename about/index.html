About aeiou.now

Let people know what is happening in the world, how different countries see it, what locals are discussing, and where they can go nearby.

The platform is not simply a news site, and it is not a traditional forum.

It is:

  • A platform for exploring global topics in real time
  • A platform for comparing cultures across countries
  • A real-time platform for user-generated discussion (UGC)
  • A ranking of the world's trending topics
  • A Topic-driven platform for local discovery

Core flow

  1. 1Something is happening in the world
  2. 2Local research in each country
  3. 3AI / Topic Engine brings it together
  4. 4A global Topic is created
  5. 5Country branches of the Topic
  6. 6People discuss it in real time
  7. 7Heat is calculated
  8. 8Global / country / city rankings
  9. 9Nearby shops / events / places
  10. 10People actually take action
