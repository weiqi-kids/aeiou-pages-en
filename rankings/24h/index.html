  12. 12How many days to register a residence permit — and does a new address or a new job have to be reported?Taiwan's Alien Resident Certificate, issued by the National Immigration Agency, must be applied for within 30 days of entry, and an address change reported within the same 30-day window — miss it and the Immigration Act sets a fine of NT$2,000–10,000.   20. 20How many paid leave days, how overtime is paid — what differs across seven countriesTaiwan's Labor Standards Act Article 38 tiers annual leave into six bands: 3 days at six months, 7 at one year, 10 at two years, 14 a year from three to five years, 15 a year from five to ten years, and one extra day per year after ten, capped at 30 — and unused days aren't lost, since Article 38(4) requires the employer to pay wages for them. Japan's Article 39 starts at 10 days after six months and rises to a 20-day cap at 6.5 years; since 2019 employers must ensure workers with enough entitlement actually take at least 5 days, but under Article 115 anything left unused after two years simply expires — no payout. Mainland China's Regulation on Paid Annual Leave requires 12 months of continuous service to qualify, then grants 5, 10, or 15 days by cumulative tenure, with 300% of the daily wage owed for any day the employer fails to schedule (Art. 5). India's leave protection long lived only in Factories Act Section 79, covering factory workers who hit 240 days a year at one day per 20 worked; shops and services were always governed by separate, inconsistent state laws, and a national labour code only lowered the threshold to 180 days in November 2025. Indonesia's Manpower Law Article 79 grants 12 days after 12 months; the old mandatory two-month long-leave benefit after six years' service is now merely optional under the amended law. Brazil's CLT Article 130 grants 30 days, but unjustified absences during the year shrink it, and a worker may cash out up to a third of it (Art. 143).
