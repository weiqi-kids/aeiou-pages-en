Valentine's Day

🌎How the world celebrates ↓

There is no single calendar for showing romantic affection. Japan's White Day, Taiwan's Qixi Valentine's Day, Brazil's Dia dos Namorados, and India's Valentine Week attach different expectations to gifts, replies, and dates.

Quick answers

What is this topic?
There is no single calendar for showing romantic affection. Japan's White Day, Taiwan's Qixi Valentine's Day, Brazil's Dia dos Namorados, and India's Valentine Week attach different expectations to gifts, replies, and dates.
When is it?
Taiwan · August 19, 2026；India · February 7, 2027–February 14, 2027；Japan · February 14, 2027；United States · February 14, 2027；Brazil · June 12, 2027
How do places mark it?
Taiwan、India、Japan、United States、Brazil
What differs by place?
Taiwan：Taiwan often promotes the seventh day of the seventh lunar month as Qixi Valentine's Day. Restaurants and shops build dates and packages around it, but it is not a nationwide public holiday. India：In some Indian cities, young people turn February 7–14 into a run of themed days for roses, proposals, chocolate, and other gestures. It is an urban trend, not a uniform national observance. Japan：In Japan, women giving chocolate on February 14 remains a familiar custom. Honmei-choco and giri-choco distinguish romantic feelings from other relationships, although many people do not follow that script. United States：In the United States, cards, sweets, flowers, and dates all belong to Valentine's Day. Classroom exchanges make room for classmates, friends, and family as well as romantic partners. Brazil：Brazil celebrates Dia dos Namorados on June 12 with gifts, dinners, and time together. The date follows a Brazilian commercial and social calendar rather than simply copying February 14.
