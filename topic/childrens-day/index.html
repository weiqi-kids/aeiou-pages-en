Children's Day

🌎How the world celebrates ↓

Compare Children’s Day in Taiwan, Japan, mainland China, India, and Brazil: the dates, school traditions, family activities, and different ways communities put children at the center.

Quick answers

What is this topic?
Compare Children’s Day in Taiwan, Japan, mainland China, India, and Brazil: the dates, school traditions, family activities, and different ways communities put children at the center.
When is it?
Brazil · October 12, 2026；India · November 14, 2026；Taiwan · April 4, 2027；Japan · May 5, 2027；China · June 1, 2027
How do places mark it?
Brazil · Dia das Crianças；India · बाल दिवस；Taiwan · 兒童節；Japan · こどもの日；China · 六一兒童節
What differs by place?
Brazil：Brazil celebrates Children’s Day on October 12 with gifts, games, family outings, and child-focused promotions. India：India’s Children’s Day is held on November 14, with school programs, games, and conversations about children’s wellbeing and rights. Taiwan：In Taiwan, Children’s Day is commonly marked with family outings, school activities, and small gifts for children. Japan：Japan’s Children’s Day features carp streamers, samurai-inspired displays, and foods associated with wishing children strength. China：In mainland China, June 1 is often celebrated through school performances, games, and activities planned around children.
