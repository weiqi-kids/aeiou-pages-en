Coming-of-Age Rites

🌎How the world celebrates ↓

Compare coming-of-age rites and expectations in Taiwan, Japan, the United States, India, and Brazil, where legal adulthood, religious ceremonies, family recognition, and personal choice do not always align.

Quick answers

What is this topic?
Compare coming-of-age rites and expectations in Taiwan, Japan, the United States, India, and Brazil, where legal adulthood, religious ceremonies, family recognition, and personal choice do not always align.
When is it?
There is no single verified fixed date here; timing varies by place, household, or personal choice.
How do places mark it?
Brazil；China；Indonesia；India；Japan；Taiwan；United States
What differs by place?
Brazil：In Brazil, adulthood may be marked by legal age, study, work, faith, or family celebrations; there is no single national version. China：Mainland China has no single coming-of-age rite for everyone; education, work, household registration, and family responsibility can mark the transition. Indonesia：In Indonesia, adulthood and responsibility may be expressed through religion, family, education, and local ethnic rites; islands differ. India：In India, coming-of-age may connect to religious initiation, education, work, marriage, or family rites; differences between states and communities are substantial. Japan：Japan has Coming of Age Day as a public milestone; local ceremonies, formal clothing, and family photos are common, but participation remains personal. Taiwan：In Taiwan, adulthood may be marked by legal age, education, work, or family expectations rather than one national rite. United States：In the United States, adulthood is discussed through state age laws, graduation, driving, moving out, or voting; there is no single national rite.
