Dragon Boat Festival

🌎How the world celebrates ↓

Explore Dragon Boat traditions in Taiwan, mainland China, and Japan, where rice dumplings, iris symbolism, and boat races connect to different calendars and ideas of protection.

Quick answers

What is this topic?
Explore Dragon Boat traditions in Taiwan, mainland China, and Japan, where rice dumplings, iris symbolism, and boat races connect to different calendars and ideas of protection.
When is it?
Japan · May 5, 2027；Taiwan · June 9, 2027；China · June 9, 2027
How do places mark it?
Japan、Taiwan、China
What differs by place?
Japan：Japan’s May 5 holiday is now Children’s Day; iris baths, carp streamers, and kashiwa mochi carry forward seasonal symbolism. Taiwan：Taiwanese families may make zongzi, watch dragon boat races, hang herbs, and check the lunar date for the holiday. China：Across mainland China, zongzi, dragon boat races, and stories of Qu Yuan appear in many local forms and flavors.
