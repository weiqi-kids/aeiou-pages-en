Eid al-Adha

🌎How the world celebrates ↓

Eid al-Adha follows the Islamic lunar calendar, so local announcements can shift the date. Compare prayer, charity, family meals, and community life in Indonesia, India, the United States, and Brazil.

Quick answers

What is this topic?
Eid al-Adha follows the Islamic lunar calendar, so local announcements can shift the date. Compare prayer, charity, family meals, and community life in Indonesia, India, the United States, and Brazil.
When is it?
Indonesia · May 17, 2027；India · May 17, 2027；United States · May 17, 2027；Brazil · May 17, 2027
How do places mark it?
Indonesia、India、United States、Brazil
What differs by place?
Indonesia：In Indonesia, Iduladha brings mosque prayers, meat distribution, community cooperation, and family meals, with local variations. India：In India, Bakrid combines prayer, charitable distribution, and family gatherings; food and public-holiday arrangements vary by region and community. United States：Muslim families in the United States may follow a mosque or community announcement for prayer, giving, and meals; moon-sighting methods can produce a one-day difference. Brazil：In Brazil, Muslim communities often mark the day through mosque prayer, charity, and shared meals, relying strongly on local community networks.
