Graduation Season

🌎How the world celebrates ↓

Compare graduation season in Japan, the United States, Taiwan, mainland China, India, and Brazil, where school calendars, ceremonies, clothing, photographs, gifts, and the next step can differ.

When is it?

        🇧🇷Brazil

        Brazil's graduation season is December, because the school year opens in February and closes in December. Finishing secondary school comes with its own full programme: the formatura ceremony, often a Mass, the baile, and the colação de grau. It connects to the southern-hemisphere summer and the end-of-year break, so graduating, going on holiday, Christmas and New Year frequently crowd into the same stretch of weeks.Graduating in December runs into something else: the end of the school year is also the start of the whole holiday season, so the ceremony often leads straight into travel and family gatherings, with the two compressed into the same stretch.

        🇨🇳China

        Mainland China's graduation season runs June into July, overlapping the gaokao. The June exam more or less defines the whole season: for final-year students the ceremony weighs far less than the exam and the choice of university preferences, while for other year groups June is simply the end of term. School events, photographs and farewell meals cluster in early July, followed by the waiting period as admission letters arrive one by one.Because the gaokao outweighs everything else, the graduation season in mainland China reads more like a waiting period: from the end of the exam to the results, and from the results to the admission letter arriving, the blank in between is what people remember most about the season.

        jw.beijing.gov.cn ↗

        🇮🇩Indonesia

        Indonesia's graduation season falls in May and June, just before the new academic year opens in July. The in-school perpisahan, a farewell gathering, is often separate from the wisuda, the formal ceremony parents attend. Because the new year starts in July, the break is short, and the gap between graduating and enrolling somewhere new is often only a few weeks.Because the new academic year opens in July, only a few weeks separate graduating from enrolling, so families handle the farewell and the next stage's costs at once — uniforms, books and registration fees frequently all land in the same month.

        🇮🇳India

        India's graduation season follows each state's and each school's own year, mostly falling between March and May and tied to the release of board exam results. At school level, 'graduating' is usually marked by results and by where they lead rather than by a ceremony; university convocations may be held months after the term actually ends. So in India the date of graduating and the date of celebrating it are frequently two separate things.Because exam results decide both progression and subject, the season in India is also when family discussion is most intense: what to study, which state to go to, whether to retake — those decisions usually settle within weeks of the results.

        cbse.gov.in ↗

        🇯🇵Japan

        Japan's graduation season is March, because the school year ends on 31 March. The graduation ceremony is followed almost immediately by the entrance ceremony and the new year on 1 April, with only a spring break in between. The joint is tight: a diploma in March, then a new uniform, a new school and a new class in April, with company induction ceremonies falling in the same week. The second button, the farewell address and the parting songs all belong to March.The tightness of March also means the farewell has a deadline: the same class still exists up to 31 March and is recombined on 1 April, and even the homeroom teacher may be moved on by the annual staff transfer.

        laws.e-gov.go.jp ↗

        🇹🇼Taiwan

        Taiwan's graduation season is June, because the school year runs to 31 July and the second term ends in late June. Ceremonies, the moving of the tassel, the graduation song and the photographs cluster into those weeks, followed by entrance-exam results and enrolment at the next school. It is also an administrative stretch: final grades, transfer of student records and registration at the new school all压 into the same month.Beyond the songs and the yearbook, what actually cuts one stage from the next is the transcript and the offer from the next school — the ceremony is in June, but the decision was usually settled in an earlier exam.

        edu.law.moe.gov.tw ↗

        🇺🇸United States

        In the United States the graduation season falls in May and June, and it is the most heavily ceremonial of the seven places here: gowns, caps, commencement speeches, the cap toss, relatives flying in from other states, with prom and commencement together forming a whole social season. Because the school year is set district by district, graduation dates within a single state can differ by weeks. Commencement in the United States is often a family-scale event rather than merely an administrative marker.The commencement address is a genre in its own right: an invited speaker addresses the whole hall, and the text is often carried by the press afterwards. That gives the American ceremony a layer of public address turned outward, not only a rite held inside the school.

        nces.ed.gov ↗
