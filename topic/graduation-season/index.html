Graduation Season

🌎How the world celebrates ↓

Compare graduation season in Japan, the United States, Taiwan, mainland China, India, and Brazil, where school calendars, ceremonies, clothing, photographs, gifts, and the next step can differ.

Quick answers

What is this topic?
Compare graduation season in Japan, the United States, Taiwan, mainland China, India, and Brazil, where school calendars, ceremonies, clothing, photographs, gifts, and the next step can differ.
When is it?
There is no single verified fixed date here; timing varies by place, household, or personal choice.
How do places mark it?
Brazil、China、Indonesia、India、Japan、Taiwan、United States
What differs by place?
Brazil：In Brazil, formatura ranges from school parties to formal university ceremonies; family meals, speeches, and gowns vary by level. China：In mainland China, graduation varies by school level, city, and institution; photos, ceremonies, meals, and the next study or work step are common transitions. Indonesia：In Indonesia, graduation often emphasises family photos, school clothing, and collective ceremonies; locality and education level shape the form. India：In India, school, college, and university graduations differ; gowns, certificates, family attendance, and local celebrations may all appear. Japan：Japanese graduations are concentrated in spring; ceremonies, uniforms, school songs, and farewells create a strong seasonal rhythm. Taiwan：Taiwanese graduations may include uniforms, degree hoods, yearbooks, photos, and teacher thanks; timing follows the school system. United States：In the United States, caps, gowns, speeches, and campus photos are common, but high-school, college, and state practices vary widely.
