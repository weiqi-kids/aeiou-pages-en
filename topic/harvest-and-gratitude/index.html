Harvest and Gratitude

🌎How the world celebrates ↓

This guide looks at Harvest and Gratitude through dates, relationships, and local differences. It does not assume that every household or community follows the same practice.

Quick answers

What is this topic?
This guide looks at Harvest and Gratitude through dates, relationships, and local differences. It does not assume that every household or community follows the same practice.
When is it?
United States · November 26, 2026；India · January 15, 2027–January 18, 2027；Brazil · June 24, 2027
How do places mark it?
United States、India、Brazil
What differs by place?
United States：Thanksgiving places a shared meal and gratitude at the centre of a national holiday. Turkey is common, but menus, family arrangements, and views of the history are not uniform. India：Pongal is a major harvest season in South India. New rice, thanks to the sun and cattle, and family gatherings connect the festival to farming life, especially in Tamil regions. Brazil：Brazil's June festivals gather people around corn-based foods, bonfires, music, dancing, and community games. Catholic saint days and rural traditions are part of the story, so it is more than a harvest festival.
