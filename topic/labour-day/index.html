Labour Day and Labor Day

🌎How the world celebrates ↓

Taiwan, mainland China, India, Indonesia, and Brazil generally mark workers on May 1, while the United States observes Labor Day on the first Monday in September. Compare public life, labour history, and time off.

Quick answers

What is this topic?
Taiwan, mainland China, India, Indonesia, and Brazil generally mark workers on May 1, while the United States observes Labor Day on the first Monday in September. Compare public life, labour history, and time off.
When is it?
United States · September 7, 2026；Taiwan · May 1, 2027；China · May 1, 2027；India · May 1, 2027；Indonesia · May 1, 2027；Brazil · May 1, 2027
How do places mark it?
United States、Taiwan、China、India、Indonesia、Brazil
What differs by place?
United States：The United States observes Labor Day on the first Monday in September; it is both a workers’ holiday and a late-summer leisure weekend. Taiwan：In Taiwan, May 1 can be a workers’ day off and a moment to discuss labour rights, but schedules differ by occupation and employer. China：Mainland China’s May Day holiday often brings intercity travel and family outings alongside public discussion of workers and industrial development. India：India’s Labour Day activities vary by state; unions, worker groups, and local governments may hold meetings or awareness events. Indonesia：Indonesia’s Hari Buruh may include worker rallies, labour-rights advocacy, and public discussion, with different scales across cities. Brazil：Brazil’s Dia do Trabalho can include union events, public cultural programs, and family leisure, grounded in a long labour history.
