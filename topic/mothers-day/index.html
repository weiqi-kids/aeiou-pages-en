Mother's Day

🌎How the world celebrates ↓

The United States, Taiwan, Japan, and Brazil generally observe Mother’s Day on the second Sunday in May, while Indonesia marks it on December 22. Compare gifts, meals, care, and family expectations.

Quick answers

What is this topic?
The United States, Taiwan, Japan, and Brazil generally observe Mother’s Day on the second Sunday in May, while Indonesia marks it on December 22. Compare gifts, meals, care, and family expectations.
When is it?
Indonesia · December 22, 2026；United States · May 9, 2027；Taiwan · May 9, 2027；Japan · May 9, 2027；Brazil · May 9, 2027
How do places mark it?
Indonesia、United States、Taiwan、Japan、Brazil
What differs by place?
Indonesia：Indonesia’s December 22 Hari Ibu also connects motherhood with women’s public contribution, rather than focusing only on gifts. United States：In the United States, flowers, cards, brunch, and family gatherings are common, while some people use the day to recognize care work. Taiwan：Taiwanese families may express thanks through meals, cakes, flowers, or practical gifts; schools sometimes invite children to make cards. Japan：Japan’s Haha no Hi often features carnations, sweets, food, or household gifts, but time spent together can matter more than a purchase. Brazil：Brazil’s Mother’s Day commonly brings a family lunch, flowers, and gifts, with schools and shops creating themed activities.
