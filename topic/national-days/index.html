National Days

🌎How the world celebrates ↓

The seven national days commemorate different kinds of events: the United States, Brazil, Indonesia and India mark the moment of leaving colonial rule or declaring independence; mainland China marks the founding of a state; Taiwan marks an uprising that ended an empire; and Japan's National Foundation Day has no corresponding dated historical event at all, being set on a legendary enthronement.

    🇧🇷How does Brazil mark National Days?Dia da Independência do Brasil

    Brazil's Independence Day is 7 September, marking the declaration of independence on that date in 1822, and it is a national holiday under Law 662. What makes it distinctive is the process: Brazilian independence was declared by the Portuguese heir apparent, and what followed was an empire rather than a republic — so 7 September commemorates the break with the colonial metropolis, while the form of state established then is not the one in place today.

    planalto.gov.br ↗

  • October 1, 2026Local date

    🇨🇳How does China mark National Days?国庆节

    Mainland China's National Day is 1 October, marking the founding of the People's Republic on that date in 1949. The national holiday regulation gives it three days, and shifted workdays are then used to build a run of about a week — the 'Golden Week' that is one of the largest travel and spending windows of the year. Round anniversaries such as the tenth or seventieth bring a military parade; other years are marked mainly by local events.

    big5.www.gov.cn ↗

  • October 10, 2026Local date

    🇹🇼How does Taiwan mark National Days?國慶日／雙十國慶

    Taiwan's National Day is 10 October, because an uprising broke out in Wuchang on that day in 1911, leading to the end of imperial rule and the founding of the Republic of China. It is one of the commemorative days given a one-day holiday under the Act on Commemorative Days and Holidays. There is a ceremony and flag raising in front of the Presidential Office and fireworks in the evening. It is also one of the few national days here that commemorates an uprising rather than a declaration or a founding — what is marked is the overthrow, not the establishment. The tenth of October commemorates the Wuchang uprising of 1911, and its formal name is the National Day of the Republic of China. The fixed acts of the day are the flag raising and the celebration assembly in front of the Presidential Office, completed in the morning, with the route and the street closures announced in advance each year. It is a day off, so watching the ceremony by day and the fireworks by night both fit inside it — one of the few days in Taiwan that puts ritual and leisure in the same slot.

    moi.gov.tw ↗

  • February 11, 2027Local date

    🇯🇵How does Japan mark National Days?建國記念の日

    Japan's National Foundation Day is 11 February, and it differs from the other six: what it marks is not a historical event with a recorded date but the legendary enthronement of Emperor Jimmu. It was only designated a national holiday by ordinance in 1966, and its stated purpose is to reflect on the founding of the nation and cultivate love of country — note 'reflect on', because there is no identifiable founding date. The date exists and the event does not; that is what makes it unusual here.

    gov-online.go.jp ↗

  • July 4, 2027Local date

    🇺🇸How does United States mark National Days? · Independence Day

    Independence Day in the United States is 4 July, marking the adoption of the Declaration of Independence by the Continental Congress in 1776. What it commemorates is the day a document was adopted, not the end of a war or the formation of a government — the war of independence ran on for another seven years. It is a federal holiday, with fireworks, parades and barbecues, and fireworks were written into the imagination of the day from its very first year.

    🇮🇳How does India mark National Days?Independence Day

    India has two national-level days: Independence Day on 15 August marks the end of British rule in 1947, and Republic Day on 26 January marks the constitution coming into force in 1950. The first has the prime minister raising the flag at the Red Fort and giving an address; the second centres on the large parade in New Delhi with state tableaux. Independence and constitution-making are commemorated on separate days — the founding is not one moment here but two.

    dfe.gov.in ↗

  • August 17, 2027Local date

    🇮🇩How does Indonesia mark National Days?Hari Kemerdekaan Republik Indonesia

    Indonesia's Independence Day is 17 August, marking the Proclamation of 1945. It is among the most important public days of the year: schools and government offices hold flag ceremonies, while neighbourhoods run the lomba 17-an — greased-pole climbing, cracker-eating races and other street contests that turn a national commemoration into a whole-street event. That scale of 'commemoration equals neighbourhood games day' is unique among these seven places.

    kemlu.go.id ↗
