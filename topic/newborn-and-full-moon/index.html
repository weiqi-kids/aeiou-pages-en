Newborns and the First Month

🌎How the world celebrates ↓

Compare newborn and first-month traditions in Taiwan, mainland China, Japan, India, Indonesia, and Brazil, including naming, blessings, visits, food, gifts, and choices around early care.

Quick answers

What is this topic?
Compare newborn and first-month traditions in Taiwan, mainland China, Japan, India, Indonesia, and Brazil, including naming, blessings, visits, food, gifts, and choices around early care.
When is it?
There is no single verified fixed date here; timing varies by place, household, or personal choice.
How do places mark it?
Brazil、China、Indonesia、India、Japan、Taiwan、United States
What differs by place?
Brazil：In Brazil, naming, baptism, a mass, family gatherings, or monthly photos may coexist; religious rites should follow the parents’ choice. China：In mainland China, the first month, hundredth day, naming, and announcing the birth are common reference points; families celebrate differently. Indonesia：In Indonesia, traditions such as selapanan may bless a baby at a particular point after birth; ethnic, religious, and family practice varies. India：In India, newborn customs may include naming, blessings, postpartum rest, and visits; faith and community shape names and timing. Japan：In Japan, families may mark a birth with a shrine visit, naming, and congratulations; arrangements depend on the shrine, family, and baby. Taiwan：In Taiwan, a baby’s first month may bring gifts, visits, or a family meal; recovery needs and family choice shape the celebration. United States：In the United States, newborn care, birth registration, visiting rules, and naming are practical priorities; religious blessings are family choices.
