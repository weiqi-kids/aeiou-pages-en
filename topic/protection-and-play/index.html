        Japanese Setsubun often turns bean-throwing and the call for demons out and fortune in into a family game before spring. Temples and shrines may hold larger events.

        Taiwan's Ghost Month includes community offerings, household tables, and taboos that vary by family, religion, and neighbourhood. It would be inaccurate to say everyone fears or avoids the same things.
